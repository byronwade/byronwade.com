# Thorbis Network / FMS

## What I’m building

Thorbis is my ongoing work on software for service businesses. The focus is the work itself: customers, jobs, scheduling and dispatch, estimates, invoices, and communications.

My earlier project write-up describes a job cockpit that brings job context into one view, alongside development work on dispatch and time tracking.

## Public context

The public [Thorbis repository](https://github.com/byronwade/local.thorbis.com) describes business-management workflows for service companies and other trades. It provides background on the project; its older architecture notes are not a statement of the current implementation.

The current scope and release details will be documented as the work develops. [Thorbis](https://thorbis.com) is the project’s public site.
